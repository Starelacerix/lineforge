    Mini lessons

    Shape language cheatsheet

    Circles

    Use circles and curves for friendly, soft, cute, safe, squishy, or hopeful drawings.

    Squares

    Use squares and rectangles for stable, heavy, reliable, awkward, practical, or grounded drawings.

    Triangles

    Use triangles and sharp diagonals for danger, energy, drama, speed, confusion, or tension.

    Silhouette

    Squint at your drawing. If the outer shape reads clearly, the design is already stronger.

    Logo mode

    Remove details until the idea works as a tiny mark. Keep one bold shape and one memorable twist.

    Second pass

    Always make a dramatic or simpler version. Growth hides in comparison, not perfection.
